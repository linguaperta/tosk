## The present tense

Verbs end in **-ar** in the dictionary form. Take the stem (*kant-* from *kantar*, to sing) and add
the ending for the person:

| person | ending | example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| mi | -o | mi kanto |
| tu | -i | tu kanti |
| li | -a | li kanta |
| nos | -amo | nos kantamo |
| vos | -ate | vos kantate |
| los | -ano | los kantano |
