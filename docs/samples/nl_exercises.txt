# Gated samples (docs/ARCHITECTURE.md, "Grammar gating"): a mid-course learner who started every
# skill of units u1-u4 (18 skills). Each skill is sampled as the current skill of a mixed-focus lesson:
# known grammar = base + started skills + the skill and its prerequisites; at most 2 unknown words per sentence (1 in grammar skills);
# no sentence twice within a skill. Skills after unit u4 show what the learner meets on opening them.
