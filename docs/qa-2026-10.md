# Exploratory QA, October 2026

Scope: the app UI on both real course packs (`it`, `nl`), after integration (v0.2.0 preview 2), plus the new mistake review, the thumb-zone exercise layout and the black & white (e-ink) mode added in the same pass.

## How it was tested

- **Screenshot walkthrough on the real packs.** `test/qa/qa_walkthrough.dart` boots the app over the bundled `it.sqlite` and `nl.sqlite` (in-memory progress), loads Roboto and the Material icons, and saves a PNG of every step: `QA_OUT=<dir> flutter test test/qa/qa_walkthrough.dart` (about 600 screenshots). It covers:
  - map (fresh and after practice), the long-press sheet with grammar note and word list, a lesson with every feedback state, summary with XP and tier changes, the map afterwards;
  - every exercise type through the lab, on both courses, answered wrong and right;
  - progress, verb collection, mistakes, settings, lab, non-stop start;
  - dark mode at 320×640 with text scale 1.3 and the source language set to Dutch;
  - desktop 1280×800 (Italian course, Dutch translations);
  - a synthetic 10-unit, 30-skill course at 320, 400 (text 1.3) and 1280 px;
  - black & white at 400×800, a 6" e-reader (572×772) and the Boox Note Air (1003×1337).
  I read the screenshots one by one; the issues below come from that and from the test runs.
- **The Windows app** (`flutter run -d windows --dart-define=TOSK_DATA_DIR=<temp dir>`, a new override so QA never touches the real profile): it builds, boots on the real packs into the temp profile and renders the map; no exceptions or overflows in the log. I could not click through it (no input automation available here), so the interaction coverage comes from the widget tests and the walkthrough.
- **Widget and integration tests** for everything that was fixed (see the table), the existing overflow tests at 360×640, 320×568, 768×1024 and 1280×800, and new ones: every exercise type at text scale 1.5 on both phone sizes, the 30-skill map, and black & white at seven sizes including e-readers.

## v0.3.2 user feedback

Fixes for what the user found while testing preview 3.1 (v0.3.1).

| # | Area | Feedback | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Match pairs | Going fast, something stayed selected and was counted wrong; pairs got unselected without being checked; after a wrong pair only the Italian word showed red | **Fixed.** The board is a pure state machine (`MatchBoard`): pairs resolve synchronously in either order, matched cards ignore taps, and a wrong pair's flash is only a mark. The cause of "select several times": a card tapped during the red flash *was* selected but still drawn red, so the next tap deselected it; and a card matched right after a wrong attempt stayed red until the timer. Now a tapped card stops flashing at once, and each flash timer clears only its own pair (reduced motion: the marks stay until the next tap, no timer). A wrong pair marks both cards; after checking both stay marked and a "Mixed up" list names the pairs. `MatchAnswer.wrongPairs` records the right side; the stored answer is the wrong pairs. Seeded stress tests (`match_pairs_stress_test`) |
| 2 | Spot the error | Red when choosing the right word is confusing | **Fixed.** The error word is the answer: tapping it shows ✓ (correct) with the correction; tapping another word shows that one ✗ and the error word ✓ |
| 3 | Map | A tap should first explain the skill and the learner's level | **Done.** A tap opens the skill sheet: description, tier, strength, last practice, what the next tier needs ("Learn 6 more words to reach Bronze", "Get every word to 85% recall and 7-day stability for Silver — 4 words to go"), then Start lesson (focus, length), Test out, the grammar note and the word list (collapsible). Locked skills keep the recommendation |
| 4 | Progress | Level, badges and the rest should be per language; only goal, streak and freezes account-wide | **Done.** XP, level, challenges, challenge and level badges, tiers, verbs, mistakes, tough words, non-stop records and session summaries are the active course's; the daily goal, streak, freezes, streak badges and the heatmap ("all courses") are account-wide. Still derived from the log (two replays) |
| 5 | Map | The course selector on the map is not needed | **Done.** The map shows the course title only; courses are switched, added and removed in Settings. A TODO on the Settings course rows marks where the planned "update available" indicator goes |
| 6 | Practice | Non-stop and every practice mode should progress the skills | **Verified and fixed.** Integration tests over the real engine and an on-disk database show non-stop alone introduces every facet in skill order and unlocks the map, and that lab, fading, mistake review, tough words, test out and placement all move tiers, strength and course XP. Fixed: a new facet that can't be built is skipped instead of blocking all later new facets in a non-stop run; the player also refreshes progress when its route is removed without Quit/Stop |
| 7 | Exercises | Exercise types aren't explained (transform was unclear) | **Done.** A first-time intro per type (title, what to do, an example from the course's own data, Got it), shown once (`intro.seen`, account-wide), re-openable with (?) in the player's app bar |
| 8 | Transform | "Transform" is unclear | **Done.** "Change the tense" / "Change the person", with a prominent from → to banner in the course's labels (`present → present perfect (passato prossimo)`, `io → noi`); "Change tense or person" in the Lab and lists |
