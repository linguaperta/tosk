# Exploratory QA, October 2026

Scope: the app UI on both real course packs (`it`, `nl`), after integration (v0.2.0 preview 2), plus the new mistake review, the thumb-zone exercise layout and the black & white (e-ink) mode added in the same pass.

## How it was tested

- **Screenshot walkthrough on the real packs.** `test/qa/qa_walkthrough.dart` boots the app over the bundled `it.sqlite` and `nl.sqlite` (in-memory progress), loads Roboto and the Material icons, and saves a PNG of every step: `QA_OUT=<dir> flutter test test/qa/qa_walkthrough.dart` (about 600 screenshots). It covers:
  - map (fresh and after practice), the long-press sheet with grammar note and word list, a lesson with every feedback state, summary with XP and tier changes, the map afterwards;
  - every exercise type through the lab, on both courses, answered wrong and right;
  - progress, verb collection, mistakes, settings, lab, non-stop start;
  - dark mode at 320×640 with text scale 1.3 and the source language set to Dutch;
  - desktop 1280×800 (Italian course, Dutch translations);
  - a synthetic 10-unit, 30-skill course at 320, 400 (text 1.3) and 1280 px;
  - black & white at 400×800, a 6" e-reader (572×772) and the Boox Note Air (1003×1337).
  I read the screenshots one by one; the issues below come from that and from the test runs.
- **The Windows app** (`flutter run -d windows --dart-define=TOSK_DATA_DIR=<temp dir>`, a new override so QA never touches the real profile): it builds, boots on the real packs into the temp profile and renders the map; no exceptions or overflows in the log. I could not click through it (no input automation available here), so the interaction coverage comes from the widget tests and the walkthrough.
- **Widget and integration tests** for everything that was fixed (see the table), the existing overflow tests at 360×640, 320×568, 768×1024 and 1280×800, and new ones: every exercise type at text scale 1.5 on both phone sizes, the 30-skill map, and black & white at seven sizes including e-readers.

## Findings

| # | Area | Issue | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Player | Exercise controls sat right under the prompt, leaving the lower half empty; hard to reach one-handed (user feedback) | **Fixed.** `ExerciseLayout`: prompt on top, options/tiles/grid/buttons at the bottom of the screen for all 9 types; centered 640 px column on desktop. `thumb_zone_test` |
| 2 | Player | At 320×568 and text 1.5 the feedback banner (long expected answer) overflowed the screen and hid Continue | **Fixed.** The banner is capped at 60 % of the height; details scroll, Continue stays pinned |
| 3 | Player | Flashcard with reduced motion: revealing the back threw "RenderAnimatedSize was mutated in its own performLayout" (zero-duration `AnimatedSize`) | **Fixed.** No `AnimatedSize` when motion is reduced. Covered by the black & white flashcard tests |
| 4 | Feedback | No room for the planned "Explain with AI" and "Flag exercise" actions | **Fixed.** An actions row (`FeedbackAction`, `extraActions`) above Continue; "Why?" moved there |
| 5 | Exercises | Option buttons ~44 px, word tiles ~40 px, grid cells 44 px tall: below the 48 px target | **Fixed.** 48 px minimum (tiles also 48 px wide). Tested |
| 6 | Conjugation grid | Person labels cut off at 84 px ("lui/lei (he…") | **Fixed.** Labels size to fit (up to 132 px, two lines) |
| 7 | Map | Edges that skip a row ran straight behind other nodes (People → Food → Article read as a chain) | **Fixed.** Such edges detour through a free lane between columns. Tested on the 30-skill course |
| 8 | Map | Edges ran through unit titles and skill titles | **Fixed.** Titles sit on the band color above the edges |
| 9 | Map | Long skill titles truncated on wide screens ("Tegenwoordige tijd van -are-werkwoo…") | **Fixed.** Cells up to 144 px |
| 10 | Map | Tapping a locked skill silently started a lesson | **Fixed.** It opens the sheet with "Recommended first: …" and Start anyway |
| 11 | Map | "What's fading" on a fresh profile only led to "Nothing to practice" | **Fixed.** Hidden until something was practiced |
| 12 | Navigation | At 320 px with large text the bottom bar labels wrapped ("Non-/stop", "Progres/s") | **Fixed.** The bar caps its text scale on narrow phones |
| 13 | Mistakes | (new feature) a flashcard mistake showed "You answered: again" | **Fixed.** "You marked it: Again (didn't know)" |
| 14 | Mistakes | Match-pairs prompts list the whole board | **Fixed.** Clamped to two lines |
| 15 | Engine | Stored prompts read "What does this mean?: ciao" | **Fixed.** No colon after an instruction ending in punctuation (`exercisePrompt`). Tested |
| 16 | Skill sheet | The modal barrier ignored the theme (always black 54 %) | **Fixed** (needed for black & white: no dimming overlay). Tested by pixel |
| 17 | Content | `ciao` multiple choice offered "hello" as a wrong meaning next to "hi" | **Remaining** (content/engine synonym data; not a UI issue) |
| 18 | Map | The map scrolls to where it was after returning from a session; fine, but a long course has no "jump to current unit" | **Remaining** (idea) |
| 19 | Map | With strongly linear prerequisites (synthetic course) many detoured edges share one lane | **Remaining**, acceptable; real courses branch more |
| 20 | Fonts | "→" in spot-the-error corrections and the transform target rendered as a missing glyph with the test Roboto subset | **Not a bug** on devices (system fonts have it); noted for the walkthrough |
| 21 | Player | After Check on a tall exercise (grid) the scroll position can leave the instruction half under the app bar | **Remaining**, cosmetic |
| 22 | QA harness | The asset bundle's cached focus-presets future hangs in a second widget test (test-only zone issue) | **Worked around** in the harness (presets overridden); the app is not affected |
| 23 | Interaction | Windows app not exercised by hand (no input automation) | **Remaining**: a manual pass on Windows and Android is still worth doing |

Checked without findings: accents and apostrophes (`l'acqua`, `perché`), the empty Dutch ik-ending shown as `–`, compound forms in grids and transform, long sentences wrapping in the sentence builder, "Watch the accent", summary XP / level-up / tier changes, dark mode, source language switch (Dutch translations on the Italian course), first-run states (no runs yet, nothing to review), loading and error states (existing tests), semantics labels on map nodes and options.
