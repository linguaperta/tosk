# Name and mascot

The code of tosk is open source under the Apache License 2.0, and you are welcome to fork it. The licence does **not** cover the project's name and identity:

- the name **tosk** and the wordmark,
- **Tosk**, the red squirrel mascot, and the rest of the brand art in `app/assets/brand/` (licensed separately under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, see `app/assets/brand/LICENSE`).

## What's fine

- Saying your project is "based on tosk" or "a fork of tosk".
- Sharing unchanged Tosk art non-commercially, for example in a blog post or review about the app (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, with attribution).
- Using the name to refer to this project.

## What isn't

- Releasing a fork, app or service under the name "tosk" or a confusingly similar name.
- Using Tosk or the brand art in a fork, or in a way that suggests your project is the official one or endorsed by it.

If you fork the app, give it your own name, icon and mascot. Questions: open an issue on <https://github.com/linguaperta/tosk>.
